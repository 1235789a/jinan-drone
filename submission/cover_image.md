## Color Palette
- **Background:** Dark navy blue (#1a1a2e) or deep purple (#16213e)
- **Accent:** Google Gemma brand blue (#4285F4) for the shield/judge
- **Risk colors:** Green (#34A853), Yellow (#FBBC04), Red (#EA4335) — matching Google palette
- **Text:** White (#FFFFFF)
- **Cards:** Subtle glass-morphism effect with semi-transparent backgrounds

## Typography
- **Title:** Bold sans-serif (Inter/Poppins), 48px — "AI Reliability Judge"
- **Subtitle:** Light weight, 24px — "Powered by Gemma 4 E2B · Safety & Trust"
- **Labels:** Medium weight, 16px

## Key Visual Elements
1. **Shield icon** (🛡️) at center representing the judge/safety layer
2. **Gemma 4 logo/wordmark** integrated with the shield
3. **Flow arrows** showing input → judgment → output
4. **Traffic light colors** (green/yellow/red) for risk levels
5. **Subtle circuit/neural network pattern** in background
